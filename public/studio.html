ASK ARTIMIST

QUICK ANSWERS

What can Artimist help with?

Architecture and interiors, visualization and motion, BIM/CAD production, Unreal experiences, and connected brand or web work. One focused service or a complete team can be engaged.

What does a project cost?

Focused starter scopes can fit the $200–1k range. Monthly studio capacity begins at $2,000. Larger projects are quoted after the brief, files and deadline are reviewed.

How quickly can you start?

Focused work can often begin within 3–7 business days after scope and source files are confirmed. Urgent deadlines are checked personally before we promise them.

What files will I receive?

Deliverables are agreed before work begins and can include final images, drawings, models, video, web assets and editable source files. Ownership and handover are written into the scope.

Do you provide local stamps or guarantee permits?

No. Artimist prepares and coordinates agreed permit-support documentation. Any required stamp, seal or certification must come from the appropriately licensed local professional; approval remains with the reviewing authority. Read the full boundary.
