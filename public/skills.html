01 / BIM

Revit

The building as one coordinated model.

Plans, sections, schedules and the services running between them all come off the same model, so a change in one view is a change everywhere. Families, worksets and shared parameters are set to your office standard before the first wall goes in.

  • +Architectural modelling to agreed LOD
  • +Families, schedules and sheet sets
  • +MEP coordination and clash review
  • +Exports to IFC, DWG and NWC

02 / DRAFTING

AutoCAD

Drawings that stay editable in your office.

Layered, annotated CAD built inside a real standard — grids, dimensions, schedules and title blocks where a reviewer expects to find them. You get the DWG, not a picture of one.

  • +Plans, sections, elevations, details
  • +Layer, lineweight and annotation standards
  • +Title blocks and sheet setup
  • +Redline turnaround and as-builts

03 / VISUALIZATION

3ds Max & V-Ray

The scene behind the frame.

Geometry, physical camera and lighting rig organised so the render is repeatable and the next view costs hours, not days. Wireframe and finished frame come from the same file.

  • +Scene assembly and organisation
  • +Physical camera and lens choice
  • +Lighting rigs and HDRI setup
  • +V-Ray materials and render passes

04 / REAL-TIME

Unreal Engine

Architecture you walk, not watch.

The project assembled as a real-time level — lighting, cine cameras and material instances — so a client changes the finish, the time of day or the route and sees it immediately.

  • +Real-time level assembly
  • +Lumen lighting and cine cameras
  • +Material instances and variants
  • +Packaged interactive builds

05 / REAL-TIME

D5 Render

A change is a frame away.

SketchUp or Revit straight into a lit scene. Sun angle, weather and materials are tuned live in front of the client rather than queued overnight — right for early review, where the answer matters more than the polish.

  • +Live scene lighting and weather
  • +Material library and scattering
  • +Fast review stills and flythroughs
  • +Direct SketchUp and Revit sync

06 / PARAMETRIC

Rhino & Grasshopper

Geometry driven by its definition.

Bay depth, screen density, panel rhythm — set as sliders rather than drawn once. The definition rebuilds the model live, then bakes into clean layered geometry for documentation or render.

  • +Parametric definitions and studies
  • +Facade and screen systems
  • +Rationalisation and panel schedules
  • +Baked geometry for Revit or Max
